Model assumptions

The cities provide a map for synthetic traffic and links. Routing maximizes delivered traffic, then favors higher-priority demand over aggregate link latency. Every crew handles one link at a time, without travel delays. Beam search compares feasible schedules over 14 hours using weighted unmet demand. It is a bounded search, with greedy retained if the search finds a worse plan. Single-link risks are measured on the starting incident before any repairs.
